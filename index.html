Case Studies

Three operations problems, what I did about them, and what came of it.

SingularityNET Ambassador Program · 2023 – present

Paying a volunteer community fairly, every month

Problem
Contributors worked across many workgroups. Their output was hard to compare, so rewards were hard to justify and people drifted off.
What I did
Designed the workgroup-led contribution and reward structure and the payment structure behind it, including an impact-based points system that rewards up to 15 contributors a month. Founded the Writers Workgroup and the Marketing Guild, and ran onboarding sessions for new members.
Result
100+ contributors paid through the structure. 200+ people rewarded through trivia and Zealy quests, and the daily Zealy program I helped design grew to 1,000+ participants.
Proof
Ambassador publication · Community hub
CurableLabs · Mar 2025 – Mar 2026

Writing down how a DAO and its SubDAO actually run

Problem
A DAO launching a SubDAO and IP-NFT projects had no shared playbook: how contributors join, who decides what, and which tools to use.
What I did
Designed a modular tooling stack and the contributor pathways and governance processes, then wrote the complete operations documentation for SubDAO and contributor processes.
Result
The processes were adopted across the DAO and its SubDAO, with one documented source new contributors can follow.
Proof
Operations documentation (GitBook)
Deep Funding · 2024

Reviewing grant proposals with accountability in mind

Problem
A community-run grants program had to decide which proposals deserved funding, and needed reviews that were consistent and held teams to what they promised.
What I did
Served on two review circles. Assessed 50+ proposals for quality, feasibility and contributor accountability.
Result
The circles allocated $400k+ in community funds. I later joined the Marketing Circle (2025–26) to explain the proposal process to new applicants.
Proof
Deep Funding post · deepfunding.ai
