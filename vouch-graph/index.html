Vouch Graph

Find contributors by who they've actually shipped with, not by who has the loudest bio. A versioned knowledge graph of who delivered what, with whom, that gets sharper with every hire.

Code · Demo video · built on HydraDB's open-source SelfGraph · by Kenichi

Does it get sharper the more it's used?

Vouch Graph (writes every outcome back)Profile search baselineOracle (knows the truth)

Metric: hire success rate, the share of tasks where the chosen person actually delivered. 5 simulated communities × 40 people × 12 tasks per round. Hidden true delivery rates; 35% of bios inflated. Outcomes come from the hidden rates, never from the method. Reproduce: python eval.py.

Who it's for

DAO ops leads, grant programs and ambassador programs that hire contributors every month. Seed it from what they already have (payout lists, GitHub co-commits, event rosters). Sold per community per month: contributor search with receipts. The trust path is what a lead can defend when someone asks “why them?”.
